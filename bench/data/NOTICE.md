# Third-party material in the written set

Tickets tagged `injected_instructions` in `written.jsonl` quote excerpts of prompts from **In-The-Wild Jailbreak Prompts** (`TrustAIRLab/in-the-wild-jailbreak-prompts` on Hugging Face, config `jailbreak_2023_12_25`), from Xinyue Shen, Zeyuan Chen, Michael Backes, Yun Shen and Yang Zhang, "Do Anything Now": Characterizing and Evaluating In-The-Wild Jailbreak Prompts on Large Language Models, ACM CCS 2024. Each such ticket's `injectionSource` names the row it quotes. Excerpts may be shortened and are followed by text written for this benchmark.
